How to Mount PikPak on Synology NAS (Full Tutorial)

Use CloudMount to mount PikPak as a local folder on your Synology NAS. Overseas high-speed storage and magnet offline resources are used directly on the NAS.

1. Why Mount PikPak to Synology NAS

PikPak is a cloud drive focused on overseas high-speed storage, supporting magnet links and torrent offline downloads with fast overseas routes and rich resources. After mounting to your Synology NAS:

  • Use overseas resources directly: movies and shows offline-downloaded by PikPak play directly on the NAS
  • Magnet offline storage: hand magnet links to PikPak for offline download, then read them on the NAS without using local bandwidth
  • Fast streaming: stable overseas routes make HD streaming smoother
  • Zero local storage usage: files stay in the cloud and use no NAS disk space

2. Installation Tutorial (3 Steps)

Step 1: Download CloudMount

Visit the official website to download the SPK package for your Synology model. All x86_64 Synology models are supported.

Download from Official Website →

Step 2: Install the Package

  1. Open the Synology DSM management interface
  2. Go to Package Center
  3. Click "Manual Install" in the top-right corner
  4. Select the downloaded SPK file
  5. Click Next, accept the agreement, and finish the installation

Step 3: Add PikPak

  1. Open CloudMount
  2. Click "Add Cloud Drive"
  3. Select "PikPak"
  4. Scan the QR code to log in with the PikPak app
  5. Set the mount path and click OK
Done! Open File Station and you will see all your PikPak files, including magnet offline-downloaded resources.

4. FAQ

Q: Can free PikPak users use this?

A: Yes. Free users can mount and use PikPak normally. PikPak members get more storage and offline-download quotas for a better experience.

Q: How do I add magnet links to PikPak?

A: Paste the magnet link into the PikPak app or web page to trigger offline download. Once completed, files appear in the drive and are accessible on the NAS after mounting.

Q: Is PikPak download speed affected by my ISP?

A: PikPak uses overseas routes, so some ISP networks may need a proxy for stable access. When the network is reachable, PikPak itself downloads very fast.

Q: Can PikPak transfer files with Baidu Wangpan after mounting?

A: Yes. CloudMount supports 8 drives at once, and cross-drive transfer moves files directly between PikPak and other drives.
